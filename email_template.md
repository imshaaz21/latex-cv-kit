# Application email template

Generic base. On a job branch, copy this to `email.md` and fill it in for that company
and role. One email per role. Attach that branch's CV and cover letter (run `./run.sh`
on the branch). `email.md` is gitignored, so a filled-in email never reaches a public
remote by accident.

Keep it formal: "Dear Hiring Team," or the named contact, the role title in the subject,
plain text, no emojis. Mirror four to six keywords from the job post that your CV
genuinely backs up.

**To:** `<hiring contact email>`
**Subject:** Application for `<Job Title>`: `<Your Name>`
**Attach:** `<Your_Name>_CV_<Job>.pdf`, `<Your_Name>_Cover_Letter_<Job>.pdf`

Dear Hiring Team,

I am writing to apply for the `<Job Title>` position, which I found `<on your LinkedIn post / on the careers page>`. My CV and cover letter are attached.

I am a `<Your Title>` at `<Your Company>` with `<N>` years of experience `<one line of verified experience, using keywords from the post>`.

`<Optional: one line on remote setup, time zone or hours if the post asks for them.>` I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to your team.

Yours sincerely,
`<Your Name>`
`<Your Title>`
`<phone>` | `<email>`
`linkedin.com/in/<slug>` | `github.com/<username>`
